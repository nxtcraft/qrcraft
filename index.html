How to create a QR code

  1. Choose a type: URL, Text, WiFi, Email, Phone, SMS, or Contact.
  2. Fill in the details for your QR code.
  3. Pick a color and size.
  4. Click Generate QR Code.
  5. Download as PNG or copy to clipboard.

QR Code Types

  • URL — link to any website. Scan to open the page instantly.
  • WiFi — share your WiFi password. Scan to connect without typing.
  • Contact (vCard) — scan to save a contact to your phone.
  • Email — scan to open a pre-filled email compose window.
  • Phone — scan to dial a number.
  • SMS — scan to open a pre-filled SMS message.
  • Text — encode any plain text message.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QRCraft completely free?

Yes. There are no plans, no subscriptions, and no watermarks. All QR types are free with unlimited downloads.

Do QR codes expire?

No. QR codes generated by QRCraft are static — they never expire. The data is stored inside the QR code image itself, so no server is involved and nothing can go offline.

Can I change the color of my QR code?

Yes. Pick any foreground and background color. Just make sure there is enough contrast between them — dark QR on light background scans best.

What size should I use?

For digital sharing (WhatsApp, email, websites) 300px is fine. For print (flyers, menus, business cards) use 400–600px for best scan quality.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All QR codes are generated entirely in your browser. Your data is never sent to any server.
